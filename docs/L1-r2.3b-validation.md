## Corpus V2 engineering evidence

The stdlib-only generator creates separate A/B train, calibration, and held-out files using the frozen seeds. An independent verifier reconstructs every episode's post-exclusion eligibility, checks identities, ranges, timestamps, split disjointness, contextual truth pairs, file inventory, checksums, and deterministic regeneration. Its negative test replaces the actual count of a fault-affected episode with its nominal span and confirms rejection, directly reproducing the R2.3/V1 defect class.

## Scientific stop condition

The first calibration-only command was:

```sh
./l1_evaluate_r23b calibrate evaluation/r23b_corpus_v2
```

It failed before evaluating calibration episodes:

```text
R23B_ERROR unsupported training reference: evaluation/r23b_corpus_v2/machine_a_train.csv
```

Training-only inspection established the cause. Machine A's final reset-equivalent event is a timestamp regression at session 22 row 271. The remaining accepted observations span only three distinct five-minute buckets: the tail of session 22 and two buckets in session 23. R2.2 requires at least 256 accepted observations and four observed buckets after reset. The row-count condition recovers, but the time-window condition does not.

The command emitted no calibration selection. It did not open either held-out row file, either held-out episode table, `contextual_probes.csv`, or `contextual_probe_truth.csv`. Therefore:

- there is no global novelty threshold;
- no high-side or two-sided fixed comparator was selected;
- no trained checkpoint was accepted;
- no calibration-lock artifact or fingerprint exists;
- no held-out or family comparison may be reported;
- no contextual-individuality conclusion may be inferred from the committed truth labels.

## Implemented isolated scorer

`l1_contextual_novelty.c` implements the frozen integer two-sided tail equation over an immutable `L1OperationalMemorySummary`. It performs bounded 3-by-8 work, uses no allocation, clock, filesystem, network, mutable global state, or OS interface, and returns `UNKNOWN` for unsupported/corrupt references or invalid input. It has no path to `l1_step_at()`, policy, persistence, shadow learning, or an actuator.

Deterministic tests cover all eight quantization bins, exact integer scores, unseen tails, threshold equality, invalid qualities and values, short/corrupt/inconsistent references, determinism, and byte-for-byte preservation of `L1Core`, `L1Shadow`, `L1OperationalMemory`, and its summary.

## Limitations and next permissible step

- Structural corpus validity does not guarantee compatibility with bounded reset/support semantics.
- No calibration, held-out, family, contextual-probe, or regime-recovery evidence was produced.
- The synthetic labels are not real-device truth and cannot establish user understanding, machine personality, or resource benefit.
- The scorer remains a read-only research adjunct and cannot influence protected advisories.
- Continuing the question requires a new versioned preregistration and a newly frozen corpus whose verifier simulates training support before corpus commit. It may not rewrite R2.3b V2 after this outcome.
