# HMI Level 1 v0.6 validation report

## Scientific outcome

**NO-GO.** The preregistered outcome-aware shadow learner made bounded
threshold updates, but did not change any held-out forecast relative to the
training-selected static predictor. Aggregate and per-family confusion
matrices and Brier scores are identical. The relative Brier improvement is
0%, versus the frozen requirement of at least 5%; all 60 paired scenarios are
ties (`p=1.0`) and zero of five seed aggregates improve.

This is a negative result, not evidence that outcome feedback is useless in
general. It shows only that this small threshold-offset learner, its delayed
feedback schedule, and these synthetic scenarios did not improve on the
selected fixed predictor. The experiment does not demonstrate real workload
optimization or generalized intelligence and does not authorize actuation.

The 208-byte shadow object and 60.881 ns median meet the frozen per-component

## Validation, compatibility, and safety

Strict C11 normal, `NDEBUG`, ASan, UBSan, leak, deterministic replay, V0.3-V0.5
regression, persistence, policy rejection, benchmark argument, evaluator
identity, and frozen-manifest tests passed in Debian QEMU. The sanitizer-backed
V0.6 evaluation smoke test reproduced the held-out checksum and NO-GO.

Live unprivileged sampling successfully read CPU `some`, memory `full`, and I/O
`full` cumulative PSI data and published `HOLD` recommendations only. The
deterministic core still performs no I/O, allocation, or clock reads. The
shadow component has no path into published actions. `l1_core.c`, action
priority, and V2 persistence encoding are unchanged; V1 migration and V2
round-trip/corruption recovery tests pass.

A source and Git audit found no scheduler, cgroup, kernel-control, device,
firmware, network-update, or hardware-write interface, and no tracked
credentials, VM disks, backups, or compiled artifacts.

## Limitations

- Latent events, observation noise, cumulative counters, gaps, and resets are
  deterministic simulations, not independent real-workload evidence.
- One pending non-overlapping window per scenario gives few adaptation
  opportunities. The frozen learner's updates did not affect scored forecasts.
- Binary forecasts provide only coarse calibration evidence.
- The fixed predictor and learner were selected from a small training split;
  both may overfit its scenario design.
- Nanosecond timings and process RSS are compiler-, VM-, allocator-, and
  host-load-sensitive. `ru_maxrss` is a process high-water mark.
- PSI totals are cumulative Linux evidence, not proof of cause, exclusive
  ownership, user-visible harm, or benefit from a hypothetical action.
- No forecast persistence or V2 format change was introduced. Learning resets
  on process restart.
- No resource actuator was implemented or tested.
