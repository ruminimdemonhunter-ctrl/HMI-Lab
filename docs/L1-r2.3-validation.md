The scorer and evaluator satisfy their engineering and resource gates. The scientific study is nevertheless invalid because the frozen R2.2 episode tables declare `eligible_rows` before invalid rows are injected, while the R2.3 protocol requires the declared value to reconcile with post-exclusion eligibility. Six calibration and six held-out episodes per machine have this metadata discrepancy. The evaluator reports the defect and does not reinterpret or repair the frozen corpus. Consequently the held-out scores below are reproducible descriptive evidence only, and no utility conclusion is authorized.

Even if the metadata defect were ignored, the novelty scorer was worse than the strongest calibration-selected fixed comparator on both machines. This is an unfavorable result, not evidence of anomaly-detection benefit or machine individuality.

## Implementation

`l1_novelty.c` implements the preregistered integer rarity equation as a pure, fixed-time, read-only C11 adjunct. It returns `UNKNOWN` for invalid quality, invalid values, insufficient support, or inconsistent summaries. It does not update operational memory or touch the protected core, shadow learner, policy, persistent state, or an OS interface.

The bounded evaluator strictly parses the frozen row and episode files, trains A/B reference summaries from training data only, searches all 1,080 fixed-comparator candidates per machine on calibration only, selects one global novelty threshold, and requires the committed calibration lock before held-out access. Unit tests cover exact arithmetic, boundaries, quality abstention, corrupt summaries, determinism, and byte isolation. The locked replay is included in normal RC0 CI and sanitizer-backed GitHub Actions.

## Safety and limitations

- Protected core, action priority, shadow learner, R2.1 perception, R2.2 operational memory, V2 persistent state, and historical fixtures were not modified.
- Tests verify the scorer cannot mutate core, shadow, or operational-memory bytes and cannot affect published actions.
- There is no runtime feedback, action coupling, resource actuation, live PSI capture, user profiling, L2/L3 implementation, network service, or production authorization.
- The data are engineered synthetic episodes. They do not establish real-workload transfer, machine personality, actual anomaly recognition, energy savings, or resource benefit.
- The shared-probe corpus has no independently adequate machine-conditioned truth. Different A/B histories or decisions therefore cannot establish correct individuality.
- The frozen eligibility-metadata defect must be resolved only under a new preregistered corpus/protocol; it cannot be repaired post hoc in R2.3.
