## Implemented preflight

The stdlib-only offline harness provides:

- an independent strict fixture verifier for canonical schemas, IDs, event order, completed sessions, reset boundaries, actual support, provenance, separated truth, and manifest bytes;
- executable `RECENT_N` comparators for N in `{1,2,3,5,8}`, trained `STATIC_WORKSPACE`, `STATIC_GLOBAL`, and `ALWAYS_NONE` comparators;
- the real compiled, unmodified `l2_fixture_cli` as `V01_FROZEN` and as a cross-check against the independent verifier;
- one shared query-context safety wrapper for every candidate;
- a strict prediction parser and exact integer toy-loss scorer;
- deterministic selection over the entire frozen comparator grid;
- a canonical single-occurrence SHA-256 lock that binds protected source, fixtures, tools, outputs, selected settings, and the calibration digest;
- a first-open sentinel proving invalid locks cause zero future-input open attempts.

## Limitations and verdicts

This preflight establishes that the proposed scientific instrument detects its frozen known errors. The data are deliberately small and synthetic, the loss is a toy arithmetic contract, the candidate relationships are non-executable enums, and the lock is unauthenticated. Results do not establish useful prediction, individuality, real-workflow transfer, learning benefit, resource savings, or production safety. A full V0.2 study requires a separate frozen protocol and explicit authorization.
